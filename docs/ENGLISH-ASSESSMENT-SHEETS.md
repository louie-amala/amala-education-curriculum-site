learner or one per class. Layouts are shown as tables here and set as forms in the docx pack._

**Sentences to include in any report**

- This check measures reading, writing and speaking. **It does not measure listening.**
- Reading, writing and speaking are reported separately and **are not averaged**.
- _(If sampled)_ Speaking was assessed for ___ of ___ learners, chosen at random before the course
  began, and the same learners were assessed again at the end.
- _(If anything was skipped)_ ___ was not run, because ___.

> **If the anchor totals moved as much as everything else**, look at the administration before you
> believe the result — the two sittings were probably not run the same way.

# 4 · The learner profile sheet

**One per learner. Given to them, not filed. Read it through with them — most learners at this level
cannot read it alone, and that is fine.**

## What I can do in English

_Tick together. It is fine if some are not ticked. You will keep growing._

**Listening and sounds**
☐ I can hear the first sound in a word.
☐ I can hear the difference between two sounds.

**Reading**
☐ I know some letters.
☐ I can find my own name.
☐ I can read the words **I · am · from · like**.
☐ I can sound out short words.
☐ I can read a short sentence about someone.
☐ I can find what I need on a notice or a timetable.

**Writing**
☐ I can write my own name.
☐ I can copy a sentence.
☐ I can fill in a form with my name and my age.
☐ I can write true sentences about myself.
☐ I can write a short note to somebody.

**Speaking**
☐ I can say my name in English.
☐ I can say who I live with.
☐ I can say what I like.
☐ I can talk about a picture.
☐ I can answer questions about my day.
☐ I can talk about what I want to do next.

## My growth

- At the start I could ______________________.
- Now I can also ______________________.
- One thing I want to get better at is ______________________.
- I feel ______________________ about learning English.

> _For the facilitator: fill the first two lines from the learner's own two sheets, in their words,
> not in levels. "At the start you could write your name. Now you can also write five sentences."
> Never show a learner a step number or a CEFR level — it means nothing to them and it can only
> discourage._
