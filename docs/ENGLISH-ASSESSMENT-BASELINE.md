# The English Check — BASELINE

**Take this in the first session, before any teaching.** The endline paper is the same tasks in the
same order with different content — see `ENGLISH-ASSESSMENT-ENDLINE.md`.

_Answers and mark schemes are at the end and print separately. They never go in a learner's paper._

Learner name ______________________  Block ________  Date ________  Facilitator ______________

> **Say this before you start, in the language you share:** "This is not an exam. Nobody passes and
> nobody fails. It shows me what to teach you. Some parts will be too hard — leave those and go on.

Write one word from the box in each gap. There are three words you will not use.

## 1.10 — A notice _(4 marks)_

> **LEARNING CENTRE — NOTICE**
>
> The learning centre is open from Saturday to Wednesday. Classes start at 9 o'clock in the morning
> and finish at 12 o'clock. On Thursday the centre is closed. English class is on Saturday and
> Monday. If you cannot come, please tell your teacher. In the rainy season, classes may start late.
> Please wait at the gate.

1. What time do classes start? ____________
2. Which day is the centre closed? ____________

3. English class is on:
 A) Saturday and Monday  B) every day  C) Thursday  D) Sunday

4. In the rainy season, classes may:
 A) finish early  B) start late  C) move to Thursday  D) stop

## 1.12 — Nur's group _(3 marks)_

_This part is harder. If it is too hard, leave it and go on to Part 2._

> Nur Begum is twenty and lives in Camp 12. Two years ago she joined an English class, but she
> noticed that many of her friends stopped coming after a few weeks. When she asked them why, most
> said the same thing: they were too shy to speak in front of a big group.
>
> So Nur tried something small. Every Friday she invited four neighbours to sit outside her shelter
> and practise English for one hour. There was no teacher and no book. They simply asked each other
> questions and helped each other find words.
>
> At first the group was quiet. After a month the four women were talking easily, and two of them
> went back to the class. Others asked to join, so Nur now runs two groups.
>
> She says the important thing is not the English. It is that nobody laughs when someone makes a
> mistake.

1. What is the text mainly about?
 A) why English classes are difficult
 B) how Nur made a small, safe place to practise English
 C) how much it costs to learn English
 D) why teachers are important

2. Why did Nur's friends stop coming to the class? ________________________________________

3. Find one word in the text that means "afraid to speak in front of other people". __________

## 2.5 — A note to your teacher _(6 marks)_

## 2.6 — A letter _(7 marks)_

_This part is harder. If it is too hard, leave it._

Write a letter to a friend about something you learned to do. It can be anything — a skill, a
subject, cooking, a repair, a language.

Say three things: **what you learned · who or what helped you · how you felt when you could do it.**

Write 100–120 words.

_(Fourteen ruled lines)_

# PART 3 — SPEAKING

_Optional. Six minutes, one learner at a time, in a quiet corner. Same-gender pairing where it
matters. The learner may pass on anything._

| | What you say | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** _(not scored)_ | "Hello. My name is ___. What is your name?" | 30s |
| **1. About you** | your name · your age, or how long you have been here · who you live with. **Do not ask where they are from.** | 90s |
| **2. The picture** | "What can you see? Tell me about this picture." | 90s |
| **3. Questions** | "What do you do in the morning?" · "What do you like?" · "What is the weather like today?" | 90s |
| **4. Tell me more** _(only if 3 was easy)_ | "Tell me about something you want to do after this course." Then once: "Why?" | 60s |
| **5. Close** _(not scored)_ | "Thank you. You spoke English with me today." | 15s |
